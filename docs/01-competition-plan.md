# 01 — Competition Plan

## Diamond Challenge at a glance (2026–27 season)

Run by Horn Entrepreneurship at the University of Delaware. Summary below is from public
sources as of Sept 2026 — **verify everything against the official 2026–27 rules PDF** on
[diamondchallenge.org](https://diamondchallenge.org) before you rely on it.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who | High school students (ages 14–18), teams of **2–4**, plus an **adult advisor** (teacher, parent, mentor) |
| Tracks | **Business Innovation** (for-profit) or **Social Innovation** (mission-driven). You pick one at submission. |
| Registration opens | Sept 16, 2026 |
| **Submission round deadline** | **Jan 14, 2027, 5:00 pm ET** |
| Submission contents | **60-second video** (public URL; team names, concept name, problem, who it's for, solution) + **3–5 page written concept narrative** |
| Pitching round results | ~Feb 10, 2027 |
| Pitching round format | Live pitch event *or* virtual/pre-recorded (you choose at submission). **5-min pitch + 3 min judge Q&A**, or a pre-recorded pitch **≤ 5:00**. Deck ~10 slides (max 15), submitted as **PDF**. |
| Finalists announced | ~Mar 9, 2027 |
| Summit (finals) | **Apr 29–30, 2027**, University of Delaware |
| Prizes | ~$100K total pool. Per track: 1st $12,000 · 2nd $8,000 · 3rd $4,500, plus special topical awards (e.g., tech, sustainability) |

### Which track?

**Recommended: Business Innovation.** SEEN is a hardware product with a price, margins, and a
subscription — judges will expect a for-profit model, and that's where your unit economics shine.

*Alternative:* Social Innovation, if you pivot the story toward a nonprofit/low-cost model (e.g.,
devices distributed free through senior centers and Area Agencies on Aging, funded by grants).
Only pick this if your interviews show that's where the real demand is.

Also aim for any **technology / health / aging** special awards.

## What the judges are looking for

The narrative should "convey the problem, the affected customers, and the solution, highlighting
its unique value compared to existing alternatives… outline the economic viability… showcase the
team's expertise… and provide evidence of progress or initial traction." Map that directly:

| Judges want | SEEN evidence you need |
|---|---|
| Real problem | Stats on memory/hearing issues in 65+; quotes from **20+ interviews** |
| Clear customer | Two personas: the senior (user) and the adult child (buyer) |
| Solution that works | **A working prototype** + demo video (this is your biggest edge — most teams only have slides) |
| Better than alternatives | Comparison table vs. Pocket, Plaud, phone voice memos, notebooks, medical alert pendants |
| Economic viability | Bill of materials, price, margin, subscription, CAC estimate |
| Team | Who built hardware, software, business, design — and your advisor/mentors |
| Traction | Pilot results (# users, recordings made, "would you pay?" %), letters of interest from a senior center |

## Team roles (for a team of 3–4)

| Role | Owns | Key deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| **CEO / Pitch lead** | Story, customer discovery, narrative, pitch delivery | Interviews, concept narrative, pitch script |
| **Hardware lead** | Device build, enclosure, battery | Working SEEN v1 unit(s), 3D-printed case |
| **Software lead** | Firmware, server, AI pipeline, dashboard | Demo-able end-to-end system |
| **Design / Ops lead** | Brand, deck, video, budget, pilot logistics | 60-sec video, deck PDF, budget sheet |

With 2 people: combine CEO + Design, and Hardware + Software.

## Week-by-week timeline

Today is late September 2026. That gives ~15 weeks to the submission deadline.

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Sept 29 → Oct 26)
- [ ] Register team + advisor on the Diamond Challenge portal
- [ ] Read the full 2026–27 rules PDF; note any AI-use or prototype rules
- [ ] Create shared drive, this GitHub repo, a group chat, a weekly meeting time
- [ ] **Order hardware** (see `03-hardware.md`) — shipping takes 1–2 weeks
- [ ] Customer discovery round 1: **10 seniors + 10 adult children/caregivers** (`06-customer-discovery.md`)
- [ ] Online survey: aim for 50+ responses
- [ ] Contact 2–3 local senior centers / assisted living facilities about a pilot

### Phase 2 — Build the prototype (Oct 27 → Nov 30)
- [ ] Week 1: Flash firmware, record a WAV to microSD on button press
- [ ] Week 2: Run the server; upload a recording over Wi-Fi; get a transcript
- [ ] Week 3: AI summary + caregiver dashboard; "ask SEEN" questions
- [ ] Week 4: 3D-print case v1, battery, vibration feedback; full end-to-end demo
- [ ] Record a clean **demo clip** (you'll reuse it in the video and pitch)

### Phase 3 — Pilot + Submission (Dec 1 → Jan 14)
- [ ] Pilot: 5–10 seniors use SEEN for 1–2 weeks (with consent forms!)
- [ ] Collect metrics: recordings/user, summary accuracy ratings, "would you pay $X?"
- [ ] Get 1–2 **letters of support** (senior center director, geriatric nurse, doctor)
- [ ] Draft concept narrative (by Dec 20), advisor review, final (by Jan 7)
- [ ] Script, film, edit the **60-second video** (by Jan 7) — upload as public/unlisted YouTube
- [ ] **Submit by Jan 12** (two-day buffer before the Jan 14, 5 pm ET deadline)

### Phase 4 — Pitching round (Jan 15 → ~Mar)
- [ ] Build the 10-slide deck (PDF)
- [ ] Write and memorize the 5-minute pitch; practice 20+ times, with a timer
- [ ] Mock Q&A sessions with teachers/parents/local entrepreneurs
- [ ] Hardware v1.1: smaller case, better battery life, 2–3 identical demo units

### Phase 5 — Summit (Mar 9 → Apr 30)
- [ ] Update traction numbers (more pilot users, pre-orders, waitlist)
- [ ] Bring 2 working units + backup video of the demo (never trust venue Wi-Fi — see `04-software.md` offline demo mode)
- [ ] Booth/table materials if applicable: printed one-pager, a SEEN unit people can press

## Risks and backups

| Risk | Backup plan |
|---|---|
| Parts arrive late | Order in week 1; order 2 of every board |
| Prototype not done by Jan | Submission only requires video + narrative — show what works, describe the rest honestly |
| Venue Wi-Fi fails during demo | Run server on a laptop with a phone hotspot; have a pre-recorded demo video |
| AI summarizes something wrong | Show the transcript next to the summary; emphasize "family double-checks" design |
| Privacy questions from judges | Have `08-privacy-legal-ethics.md` answers memorized |
