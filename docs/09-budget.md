## Ways to fund it

- School club / CTE / STEM department budget
- Your advisor's classroom funds or a DonorsChoose project
- Local sponsors: Rotary, Lions Club, a local bank, an assisted-living company (they might
  also become your pilot site!)
- Parents / team members splitting costs, repaid from any prize money
- Keep every receipt in a shared folder — judges may ask what you've spent.
