Saltback drain node

Hangs inside a storm-drain catch basin and measures the salt washing off the road after each storm. Every part below is a real CAD solid you can open, print, simulate or buy.

STEP opens in Fusion 360, SolidWorks, Onshape, FreeCAD and simulation tools such as Ansys. STL is for slicers and meshing. GLB opens in Blender and any glTF viewer.

Bought parts are simplified to their outside shape and mounting points from the maker's datasheet. Measure the probes before printing the probe head. Prices are approximate.
