Saltback

A low-cost sensor in a storm drain measures the salt washing off the road after each storm. Saltback combines it with truck logs and the weather and tells a town how much salt each storm really needed. All data on this site is simulated.

The appStorm log, storm reports, recommendations, and "Simulate winters" for any city or type of winter. The deviceInteractive 3D model of the drain sensor: every part, exploded and section views, files and buy links. Build oneStep-by-step guide from the parts list: wiring, assembly, firmware, calibration and install.

Source code and CAD files (STEP, STL, GLB): GitHub. A Diamond Challenge project.
