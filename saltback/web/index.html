    Import storm records (CSV)

    One row per storm per route. Required columns: date, pavement_temp_F, snowfall_in, storm_hours, salt_per_pass_lb. Optional: route, lane_miles, first_pass_h, plow_every_h, temp_after_F, probe_max_mS_cm, measured_drain_salt_lb (salt the probe accounted for, route total), probe_maxed_h. Imported storms replace the example season in Season analysis; simulator runs are kept.
