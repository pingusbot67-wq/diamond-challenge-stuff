Decision rules

          
    
            
  • Needed salt: the smallest rate within 15 minutes of the best icy time any rate reaches.
    • 
            
  • Saltback's rate: needed + 10%, raised until the road is no more than 6 minutes icier than the logged result, also in a pessimistic case (faster runoff, more brine needed, faster bonding).
    • 
            
  • Next storm: cuts of at most 25%; increases not capped.
    • 
            
  • Still icy over 2 hours at 600 lb per lane-mile: salt alone can't fix it. Below 15°F: sand or a stronger chemical.
